    How It Works

    What Actually Happens When You Work With Me

    You've seen the services and the pricing. Now here's exactly what the experience looks like — from first conversation to launch to month twelve and beyond. No surprises, no hidden steps, and no jargon.

    Before We Start

    From First Chat to Go-Ahead

    01

    The Free Consultation (15–20 minutes)

    We have a relaxed call — phone, video, or in person. I ask about your business, what’s working, what’s frustrating, and what you want to achieve. I’ll flag any government grants you might be eligible for and give you honest advice on what you need (and what you don’t). If I’m not the right fit, I’ll tell you.

    02

    The Proposal (within 2–3 business days)

    You receive a clear, written document covering exactly what I’ll deliver, the fixed price, the timeline, and any grant opportunities. No surprises, no fine print. You review it in your own time — there’s no follow-up pressure.

    03

    You Say Yes (when you’re ready)

    I send a simple agreement confirming the scope, timeline, and price. Once signed, we’re off. There’s no rush — you decide in your own time.

    During the Build

    What Month One Looks Like

    If You're Getting a Website

    Week 1

    Discovery & Planning

    I ask about your customers, services, and what makes you different. I plan the site structure and begin writing copy.

    Week 2

    Design & First Draft

    You see the first version — typically the homepage and key pages. You give feedback. I refine.

    Week 3

    Full Build

    All pages designed and built. Forms, SEO, maps, accessibility, GDPR, and cookie consent configured.

    Week 4

    Launch

    The site goes live. I walk you through everything. Your care plan starts immediately.

    If You're Getting Microsoft 365

    Day 1–2

    Assessment & Planning

    I review your current email and files, recommend the right M365 plan, and plan the migration.

    Day 3–5

    Setup & Configuration

    M365 environment created — email, Teams, OneDrive/SharePoint, security (MFA, policies), and Teams Phone if included.

    Day 5–7

    Migration

    Full email migration (often overnight). I verify everything transferred correctly.

    Day 7–10

    Training & Handover

    Training session for your team. Quick-reference guide provided. I’m available for any questions.

    Getting a Digital Foundation Bundle?

    All of the above happens in a coordinated sequence — website, email, phone, security, and network configured together as one integrated system. That’s the whole point.

    After Launch

    I Don't Disappear. Here's What Ongoing Looks Like.

    Your Website Care Plan (active from day one)

    Hosting, backups, and security running automatically
    Uptime and performance monitored
    Content changes made within a business day of request
    Problems fixed — often before you notice

    Your Managed IT Plan (if applicable)

    Systems monitored proactively
    M365 user management handled
    Security patches applied automatically
    Direct access to me — not a helpdesk
    Quarterly or monthly reviews depending on plan
    Compliance posture kept current

    How Support Actually Works

    Need a content change? Send me an email or WhatsApp. I make the change — usually within a business day — and confirm when it’s done. No ticket, no portal.

    Something broken? Call me. I already know your setup because I built it. Fast, informed support without repeating yourself to a stranger.

    Proactive monitoring? I run tools that check your systems continuously. If something has an issue at 2am, I get an alert. Most months, you won’t hear from me about technical issues — because I’ve already dealt with them.
