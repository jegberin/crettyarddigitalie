    How Remote Microsoft 365 Setup Works Across Ireland

    Remote setup isn’t a compromise — for most Microsoft 365 deployments, it’s faster and less disruptive than on-site. Here’s how it runs:

    • Free discovery call: A short video call so I understand your current email, file storage, and any existing Microsoft or Google tenant. I confirm the right plan (Business Basic, Standard, or Premium) and whether Copilot makes sense for you.
    • Tenant build + hardening: I provision your tenant with EU data residency, Entra ID identity, MFA, Conditional Access, and retention policies before a single mailbox is touched.
    • Scheduled migration: Whether you’re moving from Gmail, Google Workspace, POP/IMAP, or legacy Exchange, I run the migration overnight where possible. You log in the next morning to a working inbox.
    • Remote handover + training: Screen-share training with your team covering email, OneDrive, Teams, and (if applicable) Teams Phone and Copilot. Recordings shared so new hires can onboard themselves.

    If you’re in Laois, Carlow, Kilkenny or the Midlands and want face-to-face, I’m happy to come on-site — everywhere else in Ireland runs remote-first without compromise.
