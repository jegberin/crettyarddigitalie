    Managed Hardware

    Modern Business PCs & Servers — Delivered Ready to Use, Across Laois, Carlow, Kilkenny & the Midlands

    Turn it on, log in, get back to work. Your new systems arrive fully configured, licensed, encrypted, and with your data already moved across — thanks to Windows Autopilot and Microsoft Intune device enrolment done before the kit leaves me. No setup week. No lost productivity. No IT headaches. You focus on your business — I do the tech.

    Who This Is For

    If Any of This Sounds Familiar, Read On

    Running PCs from 2018?

    Hardware that groans when you open a browser. Machines still on Windows 10 (unsupported since October 2025). Starting to crash or slow to a crawl by mid-morning.

    Shared drive on someone’s PC?

    Critical business files stored on one person’s laptop. No real backup. When they’re out sick, everything grinds to a halt. One dropped laptop away from disaster.

    Keep meaning to sort it?

    You know the setup needs an overhaul. You’ve been putting it off because the thought of picking kit, installing software, migrating data, and training staff feels like a fortnight’s worth of misery.

    The Outcome

    Systems That Arrive Ready to Work. Not Boxes You Have to Figure Out.

    Anyone can sell you a PC. The hard part is everything that comes after — licensing, setup, encryption, moving your files across, connecting it to your printer, email, Wi-Fi, and everything else you rely on. I do all of that before it reaches you.

    Productive from day one

    Your new PC or server arrives with Windows, your apps, your files, your email, your printer — everything. Log in, open your browser, you’re working. No setup wizard, no day lost to configuration.

    Your data is already there

    I handle the data migration from your old machines. Documents, photos, mail archives, bookmarks — it’s all where you expect it. You don’t lift a finger and you don’t lose a file.

    Secure from the first login

    Full-disk encryption enabled. Strong admin controls. Multi-factor authentication on your accounts. Microsoft Defender for Endpoint baseline enforced via Intune. Proper business-grade setup, not the out-of-the-box defaults that leave you exposed.

    One number when something’s wrong

    If anything ever goes sideways, you ring me. I already know your setup because I built it. No ticketing queue, no explaining your environment to a stranger, no waiting days for a callback.

    You focus on your business. I do the tech.

    What I Supply

    Compact, Quiet, Fast. Built for Work.

    Two straightforward categories, chosen to fit how small Irish businesses actually operate.

    Business Desktop

    A compact, modern PC that sits neatly behind your monitor or under your desk. Fast enough for anything a small business needs — browser work, Microsoft 365, accounting software, video calls, design tools, the lot.

    • Compact form factor (fits behind a monitor)
    • Quiet enough for a shared office
    • Significantly lower power draw than old towers
    • Multiple monitor support
    • Windows 11 Pro, properly licensed
    • Full-disk encryption enabled

    Ideal for: office staff, professional services, admin roles, anyone working across browser apps and productivity tools.

    On-Site Server & NAS

    A small-footprint server for shared files, automated backups, and light business apps. Fits in a cupboard, not a server room. Replaces that dodgy shared drive on Mary’s laptop with something proper.

    • Shared files accessible from any device
    • Automated, monitored backups
    • Multi-drive storage with redundancy
    • Fast internal networking
    • Choice of Linux, flexible NAS, or Windows Server
    • Encryption at rest, user access controls

    Ideal for: shared drives, line-of-business apps, on-site backup target, small teams that need reliable file storage without the cloud-only cost.

    I supply current-generation, business-grade hardware from trusted European suppliers — not consumer kit, not gaming rigs, not old stock. Exact specifications depend on what you actually need, which we’ll work out together.

    Security & Compliance

    Built In, Not Bolted On

    Every device I supply ships with the security controls small businesses are increasingly being asked about — by larger clients, by insurers, by auditors. Not because I’m ticking boxes, but because it’s how systems should be built in 2026.

    Full-disk encryption

    Every drive encrypted (BitLocker on Windows, LUKS on Linux). Lose a laptop? The data on it is unreadable. Recovery keys stored centrally and securely, managed via Microsoft Intune.

    Multi-factor authentication

    MFA on all administrative access and business cloud accounts. No more “password123” standing between attackers and your business.

    Automated monitored backups

    Workstations back up to the on-site NAS and/or cloud. Ransomware, hardware failure, or a deleted folder doesn’t mean permanent data loss.

    Hardened baseline + Defender for Endpoint

    Current patches applied via Intune / Autopilot. Legacy protocols disabled. Local firewall active. Microsoft Defender for Endpoint enrolled. Secure remote management set up. The defaults most suppliers leave open.

    Documented controls

    You get a written record of what’s been configured — useful when larger customers send you supply-chain security questionnaires under NIS2 or ask about EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) posture.

    Supports your compliance obligations

    Technical controls that support your GDPR, NIS2, and (where relevant) DORA obligations. I’ll tell you plainly what this does and doesn’t cover.

    Honest disclaimer: These controls support your NIS2, GDPR, and DORA obligations — they don’t replace the policies, incident response procedures, and organisational measures you also need. Compliance is never just about hardware. If you want the full picture, see the cybersecurity & compliance page.

    How It Works

    From First Chat to Fully Operational

    One conversation, one quote, one delivery day. Here’s how a typical hardware refresh runs.

    01

    Free discovery call

    We talk about what you’re running now, what’s causing you grief, how many people need kit, what apps you rely on, and where you want to be. About 30 minutes.

    02

    Fixed-price proposal

    Within 48 hours I come back with a fixed-price proposal: exactly what you’re getting, how much it costs, and when it’ll be delivered. No surprises, no “plus VAT on request” nonsense.

    03

    You approve, I order

    Once you’re happy, I place the hardware order and start preparing your configuration via Windows Autopilot. You get a delivery date up front.

    04

    Build, harden, migrate

    I build each system: install Windows properly licensed, apply all updates, enrol into Intune, configure Defender baselines, install your apps, and migrate your data from old machines — all before it ever reaches your office.

    05

    On-site install day

    I come to you, set up the equipment, connect it to your network and peripherals, and run through the basics. Your team starts working on the new kit the same day.

    06

    Handover & quick training

    A short walkthrough so your team knows what’s changed, where files are, and how backups work. Written summary left with you for reference.

    07

    Ongoing support (optional)

    Add Hardware Care or a Managed IT plan for ongoing monitoring, updates, and priority support. One phone number forever.
