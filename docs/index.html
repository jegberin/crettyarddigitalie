    If Customers Can't Find You Online, They're Finding Your Competitors

    Someone in your area just searched Google for exactly what you do — "plumber Carlow," "furniture maker Laois," "cleaner near me." They didn't find you. They found a competitor whose website looked professional, had a proper email address, and had a phone number they could tap. That competitor got the job. You didn't even know you were in the running.

    Potential customers find your competitors on Google instead of you
    A Gmail or Yahoo email address makes you look like a hobby, not a business

    Word-of-mouth alone eventually slows down — and you have no backup plan
    New EU regulations mean your technology may need to meet legal standards

    Instead of buying a website, email, and IT support separately, bundles give you everything as one integrated package — with one setup cost and one predictable monthly fee.
