    AI Readiness & Microsoft Copilot

    Microsoft 365 Copilot & AI Readiness for Small Business — Practical, Not Hype

    You've heard about AI, ChatGPT, and Microsoft 365 Copilot. You're not sure what it can actually do for a business like yours — or where to start. I help small businesses cut through the noise, navigate the EU AI Act in plain English, and implement Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Chat, or (where it fits) Copilot Studio and Azure OpenAI — with data access and permissions configured properly. Genuine productivity gains, no buzzwords.

    In Practice

    Forget the Hype. Here's What AI Does for a Small Business.

    AI in a small business isn't about robots or replacing people. It's about automating the time-consuming admin tasks that eat into your working day.

    Draft Emails in Seconds

    Tell Copilot what you want to say. It writes a professional first draft. You review, tweak, and send — saving 15–30 minutes per email-heavy session.

    Summarise Documents

    Instead of reading a 20-page report, Copilot gives you a concise summary with key points and action items.

    Create Presentations

    Generate a first-draft PowerPoint from a Word document, an email thread, or a simple brief. You refine instead of building from scratch.

    Answer Business Questions

    “What were our top services last quarter?” “Summarise the last three emails from this client.” Copilot works with your Microsoft 365 data to give instant answers.

    Automate Repetitive Admin

    Formatting spreadsheets, drafting agendas, categorising email — tasks that take 20 minutes now take 20 seconds.

    Get Real Hours Back

    This isn’t theoretical. Businesses using Copilot consistently report getting 5–10 hours per week back from admin tasks.
