    Microsoft 365 for Small Business — Professional Email, Teams Phone & Cloud Tools, Set Up Properly

    Stop losing emails, sharing files by USB stick, or sending quotes from a Gmail address. I set up Microsoft 365 for small businesses across Ireland so your email, file storage, and collaboration tools work securely from anywhere — and with Teams Phone, your business calls work through the same system. One setup, one provider, one bill.

    Key Benefits

    Seven Ways to Make Your Business Run Better

    Professional Email

    Send and receive emails from you@yourbusiness.ie — not a personal Gmail or Yahoo address. Every email builds credibility. 50GB+ mailbox, shared calendars, and contacts.

    Cloud File Storage

    Quotes, invoices, photos, documents — stored safely in the cloud, accessible from any device. No more USB sticks, no more lost attachments.

    Team Collaboration

    Microsoft Teams for chat, file sharing, and video calls — whether your team is in the office, on site, or at home. One app for all communication.

    Business Phone System

    Teams Phone replaces your landline with a proper business number that works from your mobile, laptop, or desk phone — integrated with your calendar and contacts.

    Shared Calendars

    Book meetings, check availability, and set reminders without endless back-and-forth texts and phone calls.

    Work from Anywhere

    Full access to everything from any device, securely. Whether your team is at a desk, on a building site, or at the kitchen table.

    GDPR-Compliant from Day One

    I configure your environment with EU data residency, Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD) identity, data retention policies, Multi-Factor Authentication, and encrypted communications — so your setup meets GDPR requirements from the start. Business Premium adds Intune device management if you need it.

Your team gets a proper business number that works from any device, anywhere — all integrated with your calendar, contacts, and email.

    • Proper business number (or keep your existing one)
    • Calls from mobile, laptop, tablet, or desk phone
    • Voicemail-to-email transcription
    • Call forwarding, auto-attendants, and call queues
    • No separate phone company needed
